GCash Payment Instructions
How to Pay via GCash:
  1. Open your GCash or PayMaya app
  2. Contact the seller to get their GCash/PayMaya number
  3. Send money to the seller's account for ₱{{ transaction.price }}

Important Information

Make sure to confirm the seller's GCash/PayMaya number directly through chat first. Save the transaction receipt as proof of payment.

Scam-Prevention Reminder

E-wallet transfers can be hard to reverse. Do not send funds outside the agreed transaction context, and keep screenshots of reference numbers, chat confirmations, and any delivery tracking updates.
