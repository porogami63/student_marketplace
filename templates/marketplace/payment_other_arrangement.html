What is a Custom Arrangement?

A custom arrangement is any payment method not listed in the standard options. This could include payment plans, delivery arrangements, or other agreements negotiated directly with the seller.

Common Custom Arrangements:
Installment payment plan with agreed dates and amounts
Payment via cryptocurrency or alternative digital wallet
Delayed payment after item delivery to another location
Trade/exchange of items instead of cash payment
Payment collection through a mutual friend or intermediary
Important Reminder

The seller will need to review and confirm these arrangement details. Make sure both parties are in agreement on all terms before submitting. Custom methods carry higher scam risk because they are less standardized, so keep proofs, references, and delivery updates documented in chat.
